# Original visual assets

`ShadowProgress/Assets.xcassets/ExpeditionHero.imageset/ExpeditionHero.jpg` is original artwork created with the built-in image generation tool, then saved as an optimized JPEG for the app. It is not a game screenshot or an official Tomb Raider asset.

Prompt: “Premium native iPhone companion app landscape header artwork. An original atmospheric cinematic matte illustration inspired by the exploration atmosphere of Shadow of the Tomb Raider: dense emerald Peruvian jungle, towering ancient stepped stone temple emerging from mist, weathered limestone carved stones, warm golden eclipse low in the sky, deep teal cenote water in foreground. Rich layered painterly realism, restrained cinematic lighting, tactile stone and lush vegetation. Wide landscape composition with main temple in right half and quieter misty dark foliage at left for optional text overlay. Sophisticated dark emerald, jade, muted warm gold and ivory highlights. No characters, no text, no logos, no game UI, no border.”

The standard, dark, and tinted app icons are rendered by `Scripts/GenerateAppIcon.swift` from original geometric paths. The Atlas route diagram is drawn in SwiftUI Canvas. No third-party game logos or map images are redistributed.
